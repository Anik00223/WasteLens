Decision ladder

Reject first. Then ask if the bin is obvious.

model

Dual head on a frozen backbone

  • Input 224×224, normalized with (x / 127.5) − 1.
  • Four-class softmax: recyclable, organic, hazardous, general trash.
  • Sigmoid rejection head: probability the image is outside scope.
  • TensorFlow.js 4.22.0, WebGL, no upload.
gates

The order that actually decides

  • Unsupported when the rejection score is at least 0.0702.
  • Uncertain when the top class is under 0.60, or the margin under 0.50.
  • Otherwise the top class is the predicted bin.

Known misses

  • A large centered item can light up several overlapping windows.
  • A center window can bridge two separate objects.
  • Busy countertops can push a real item over 0.0702.
  • Isolated non-waste can still look like a candidate.

Not in this model

True bounding boxes would need instance labels and a detector. That is future work. This page does not pretend the eight windows are one.
